Common Questions

CAC registration, answered

How long does CAC registration take?

Business names are typically completed within a few working days once documentation is complete. Company incorporation usually takes one to two weeks depending on CAC processing times and name approval. We keep you updated at every stage.

What is the difference between a Business Name and a Limited Company?

A business name is simpler and cheaper but does not separate you legally from the business. A limited company (LTD) is a separate legal entity — it protects your personal assets, looks more credible to banks and corporate clients, and is required for many contracts and grants. We'll advise you on which fits your situation during the free consultation.

Can I register from outside Abuja — or outside Nigeria?

Yes. The entire process is handled remotely nationwide, and we regularly register companies for Nigerians in the diaspora. Everything can be done via WhatsApp and email.

What happens after registration?

We guide you through obtaining your TIN, opening a corporate bank account, and staying compliant — including annual returns filing so your company is never marked inactive. HPS can also handle your bookkeeping and tax registration as your business grows.

Can HPS also register my company in the UK or US?

Yes. We provide UK company registration and US LLC registration with full documentation support — ideal if you serve international clients or need foreign invoicing and banking. Ask us about it during your consultation.
