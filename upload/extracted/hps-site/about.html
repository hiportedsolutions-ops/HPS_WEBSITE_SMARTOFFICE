Our Story

From accounting expertise to enterprise solutions.

HPS began with a simple observation: too many good businesses fail — not because the idea was weak, but because the systems around it were. Poor records, missed compliance, no strategy, no structure.

We built HPS to close that gap. Today we combine practical business knowledge, accounting expertise, technology and strategic advisory to help organizations improve performance, strengthen compliance and grow for the long term — in Nigeria and internationally.

We don't just solve business problems. We help entrepreneurs build systems that create sustainable growth, improve profitability, and position them for long-term success.

VisionTo become Africa's most trusted business growth and professional services partner, helping businesses and entrepreneurs compete globally.
MissionTo empower entrepreneurs, organizations and institutions with practical business solutions, financial intelligence and innovative technologies that promote sustainable growth.
